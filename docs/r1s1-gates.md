# R1 and S1: the GR1 and GS1 tables

- Date: 2026-10-20 (R1/S1 lane INT). Main at `e47543f` (every R1/S1 lane merged) plus lane INT's branch `r1s1/int`.
- Where the gates come from: [roadmap.md](roadmap.md#r1--recreate-mode-34-weeks) (GR1, GS1) and [phase-2.md](phase-2.md#the-gr1-and-gs1-checks) (the checks, who runs each, when). The format follows [p1b-g1.md](p1b-g1.md).
- Status words: **passed** (measured or green, with the source), **pending owner** (your eyes, ears, hands or a decision; [owner sheet 3](r1s1-owner.md)), **pending friends** (your friends' rounds), **pending CI** (a workflow that has not run green on the tree yet), **open** (not done; the reason is given). Nothing is failed.
- Short version: every automated check passes. **GR1 waits for rounds**: yours and your friends', which need the friend build (one `release.yml` run). **GS1 waits for your preset pick, your four G1 sounds and the real-display check.**
