# R3a: the table of record

- Date: 2026-10-22 (R3 lane INTa). Main at `b25c609` (every R3a lane merged: S0s, DS, DEG, LT, FE, TP, OBJ, EVK, F1S, FK3, SK3, SK3M, CLC, PUI) plus lane INTa's branch `r3/inta`.
- Where the gate comes from: [roadmap.md](roadmap.md#r3--sound--patch-610-weeks) (R3: create from sound in the CLI and the plugin; every result in under 5 minutes) and [phase-3.md](phase-3.md#the-gr2-and-gr3-checks): the rows **GR3 clause 3** ("every result in under 5 minutes": a cap guarantees it, so it is gated on time to the first candidate, at most 15 s, and no xruns while a search runs in the DAW) and **R3a ships** (the full gate, REAPER, leak; the arm-A baseline; your 5-sound check), with freeze F2s's row (the cap, cancel within one batch, `process-stats --gate g1`, the first candidate, `validate`, `reaper-test`). GR3's clauses 1 and 2 are R3b's (lanes EVb and INTb add them here). The format follows [r2-gates.md](r2-gates.md).
- Status words: **passed** (measured or green, with the source), **pending owner** (your ears, hands or a decision; [owner sheet 5](r3-owner.md)), **open** (not done; the reason is given), **deferred** (to R3b, with the reason). Nothing is failed.
- Short version: **every automated R3a check passes.** The full gate, REAPER (default and `create` bundles) and the leak gate (both) are green on INTa's branch; a job's first candidates arrive in under a second (the bar is 15 s), the cap holds to within 0.13 s, cancel and unload take 10–19 ms, and G1 passes with create jobs running; the arm-A baseline is in (29 of 40, median 0.40 JND). The k-NN bank rebuilds bit for bit and a release run with its new `create` input ships it beside the bundles (rehearsed on Linux). **What is left is yours:** the 5-sound check (S5), REAPER's media-item drag (open check), and a yes for Create in the friend build (B4; default not yet).
