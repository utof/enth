# Glide

Glide makes the pitch slide from the last note to the next, as a voice or a fretless bass does.

- [Glide](enth:voice.glide): the time to slide from the previous note's pitch to the new one; 0 is off.
- [Glide mode](enth:voice.glide_mode): glide on every note, or only on notes played legato, while the last one is
  still held.

It works best in mono or legato mode, where one note follows another.
