# Playing sources

How you play can be routed like a modulator:

- **Velocity**: how hard each note is struck. On the cutoff, hard notes come out brighter.
- **Key**: which note it is. On [Pan](enth:voice.pan), low notes sit left and high notes right.
- **Mod wheel**: a control you move by hand while you play.

[Pan](enth:voice.pan) places each voice from left to right.
