# Negative depth

A route's depth can go below zero. Then the envelope pushes its knob **down** instead of up.

- **Positive:** the cutoff starts above the knob and falls back to it: the sound closes.
- **Negative:** the cutoff starts below the knob and rises to it: the sound opens.

The [cutoff knob](enth:filter1.cutoff) is still where the sweep ends; only the direction flips.
