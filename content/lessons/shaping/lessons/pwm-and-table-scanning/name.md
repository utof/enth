# PWM and scanning

An LFO can move an oscillator's own shape.

- [Pulse width](enth:osc1.pw): the width of the pulse wave; 50 % is a square. Modulated, it gives **PWM**, a shimmering,
  chorus-like pulse.
- [Position](enth:osc1.position): where in the wavetable to play, from its first frame to its last. Modulated, the
  table **scans**.
