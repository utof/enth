# Wobble and sweeps

An LFO routed to the [cutoff](enth:filter1.cutoff) moves the brightness: [the route](enth:lfo1->filter1.cutoff)'s
depth sets how far, lfo1's [Rate](enth:lfo1.rate) how fast.

- A few hertz: a **wobble** or wah.
- Under one hertz: a slow **sweep**, heard over a long note.

On the cutoff, a depth of 10 % swings about an octave each way.
