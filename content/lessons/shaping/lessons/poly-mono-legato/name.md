# Voice mode

[Voice mode](enth:voice.mode): poly plays chords; mono and legato play one note at a time (legato slides without
retriggering).

- [Polyphony](enth:voice.polyphony): how many notes can sound at once in poly mode; extra notes steal the oldest
  voice.
- **Mono** starts each new note afresh. **Legato** carries the sound on when a note overlaps the last one.
