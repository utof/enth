# Vibrato

An **LFO** (low-frequency oscillator) is a slow wave that moves a knob. Routed to an oscillator's
[Fine](enth:osc1.fine) tuning, it makes **vibrato**.

- [Rate](enth:lfo1.rate): cycles per second, about 5 Hz for vibrato, under 0.5 Hz for slow sweeps.
- **Depth** of [the route](enth:lfo1->osc1.fine): how far the pitch swings. On Fine, 12.5 % is plus and minus 25
  cents, a quarter of a semitone.
