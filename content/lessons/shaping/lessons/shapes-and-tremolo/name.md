# Shapes and tremolo

Routed to an oscillator's [Level](enth:osc1.level), an LFO makes **tremolo**: the loudness rises and falls.
[Shape](enth:lfo1.shape) is the waveform of the modulation:

- **Sine**: a smooth wave, natural vibrato and tremolo.
- **Square**: jumps between two values, trills and gating.
- **Saw down**: drops then rises, repeating plucks.
- **Sample and hold**: a new random step every cycle.
