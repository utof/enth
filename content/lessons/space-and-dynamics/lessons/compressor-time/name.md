# Compressor timing

- [Attack](enth:comp1.attack): how fast it reacts to a rise in level. Slower lets the click through.
- [Release](enth:comp1.release): how fast it lets go after the level falls. Fast release breathes; slow holds on.
- [Mix](enth:comp1.mix): how much compressed sound you hear. Lower blends in the dry sound (parallel compression).
- [Knee](enth:comp1.knee): how gently it starts around the threshold; 0 dB is a hard knee.
