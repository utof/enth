# The compressor

A compressor turns the sound down whenever it gets louder than a level you set.

- [Threshold](enth:comp1.threshold): where it starts; lower compresses more.
- [Ratio](enth:comp1.ratio): how hard. At 4:1, 4 dB over the threshold comes out 1 dB over.
- [Make-up](enth:comp1.makeup): gain after it, to bring the level back up.

Here each note's click gets through, and the ring after it is held down.
