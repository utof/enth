# Phase and seeds

The phase is where in its cycle a note starts. Alone, an oscillator sounds the same either way; two at one pitch
blend differently.

- [Phase mode](enth:osc1.phase_mode): free starts each note at random; reset at the
  [start phase](enth:osc1.phase); seeded from the [seed](enth:osc1.seed).
- At 180 degrees, two saws cancel half their harmonics: thin, and an octave up.
