# The reverb's tone

These shape the tail, not the note.

- [High cut](enth:reverb1.high_cut) and [Low cut](enth:reverb1.low_cut) remove highs or lows from the reverb:
  darker and softer, or cleaner in the bass.
- [High damping](enth:reverb1.high_damp) and [Low damping](enth:reverb1.low_damp): how much faster highs or lows die
  away than the rest.
- [Early reflections](enth:reverb1.early): the first echoes, which tell the ear the room's size.
- [Detune](enth:reverb1.detune): a wobble in the tail that smears metallic ringing.
