# Macros

A [macro](enth:macro1) is a knob for the whole sound, from 0 to 1. On its own it does nothing: its routes give it
jobs, like [this one to the cutoff](enth:macro1->filter1.cutoff).

- Each route's **depth** says how far the macro pushes its knob at 1.
- A **negative** depth pushes the other way: down as the macro goes up.

One macro can drive many knobs, so one turn changes the sound in several ways.
