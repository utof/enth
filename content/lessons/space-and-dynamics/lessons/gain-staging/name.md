# Gain staging

Every level on the way out adds up. Past 0 dBFS the output clips.

- The [master level](enth:master.level) sets how loud the whole patch leaves enth.
- The sources' levels, like [this oscillator's](enth:osc1.level), set their balance. Turned down together, the
  sound stays the same, only quieter.

Keep the peaks a little under 0 dBFS, and compare sounds at the same loudness.
