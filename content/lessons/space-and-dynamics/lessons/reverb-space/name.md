# Reverb

Reverb puts a sound in a space: after each note comes a tail of echoes too dense to tell apart.

- [Wet](enth:reverb1.wet): the level of the tail. More sounds further away; -60 dB is off.
- [Dry](enth:reverb1.dry): the untouched sound. Turn it off for only the reverb.
- [Room size](enth:reverb1.room): small rooms to large halls.
- [Decay](enth:reverb1.decay): how long the tail takes to die away.

The tail is clearest after short notes.
