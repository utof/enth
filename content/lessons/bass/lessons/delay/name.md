# Delay

A delay plays the sound again after a moment, then again, each echo quieter.

- [Sync](enth:delay1.sync) and [Beats](enth:delay1.beats): the time between echoes in beats of the tempo;
  1/8 dotted is the classic bounce.
- [Feedback](enth:delay1.feedback): how many echoes come back.
- [Mix](enth:delay1.mix): how loud the echoes are; the dry sound always passes.
