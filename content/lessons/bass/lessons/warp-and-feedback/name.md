# Feedback and warp

Two ways to grow harmonics from one oscillator, no second one needed.

- [Feedback](enth:wt1.feedback): wt1 bends its own phase with its output: a sine turns towards a saw, then
  a rough, noisy edge.
- [Warp](enth:wt1.warp) and [Warp amount](enth:wt1.warp_amount): bend, squeeze, sync or quantize the wave's
  cycle before it is read. At 0 % the wave is untouched.
