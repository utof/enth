# Taking a riddim wobble apart

A wobble the way riddim makes it: a saw through a resonant low-pass, wobbling at 1/8, hard-clipped and squashed by
OTT. Everything in it comes from this pack's earlier lessons.

This lesson rebuilds it part by part:

1. The distortion
2. The filter
3. The wobble (the synced LFO)
4. OTT

Each step plays the sound without and with one part, then you rebuild that part by ear.
