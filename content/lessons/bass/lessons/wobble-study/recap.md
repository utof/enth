# What made the wobble

1. **The distortion.** Hard clipping with 15 dB of drive.
2. **The filter.** A low-pass at 500 Hz with 45 % resonance.
3. **The wobble.** lfo1 on the cutoff, locked to 1/8.
4. **OTT.** 70 % depth, both directions.

Change the beats from note to note and the drop starts talking.
