# OTT

OTT splits the sound into low, mid and high bands and squashes each from both sides: quiet parts come up,
loud parts go down. A dull bass turns dense, bright and loud-sounding, which is why bass music puts it after
the distortion.

- [Depth](enth:ott1.depth): how hard all three bands are squashed; 100 % is the full sound.
- [Upward](enth:ott1.upward) and [Downward](enth:ott1.downward): lift the quiet parts, push the loud ones.

OTT sounds louder and better at first listen: [Mix](enth:ott1.mix) at 0 % is the honest before.
