# Phaser

A phaser sweeps a set of notches up and down through the sound: a hollow, moving whoosh, slow for space,
locked to the beat for riddim.

- [Rate](enth:phaser1.rate), or [Sync](enth:phaser1.sync) with [Beats](enth:phaser1.beats): how fast the
  notches sweep.
- [Depth](enth:phaser1.depth): how far they sweep.
