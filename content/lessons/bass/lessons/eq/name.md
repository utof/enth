# EQ

An EQ turns frequency bands up or down. In bass music it clears room: the sub's lows off the mid bass, the
box out of the low mids, bite in the presence.

- [Low cut](enth:eq1.low_cut) and [High cut](enth:eq1.high_cut): remove everything below or above.
- [Low gain](enth:eq1.low_gain) and [High gain](enth:eq1.high_gain): shelves, weight and air.
- [Bell 1](enth:eq1.bell1_gain) and [Bell 2](enth:eq1.bell2_gain): boost or cut a band.
