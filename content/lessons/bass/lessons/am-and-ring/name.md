# AM and ring mod

An audio route into wt1's [AM](enth:wt1.am) swings its level with another oscillator.

- [AM](enth:wt1.am) at 100 %: the note stays and the modulator adds a flutter or, when fast, new tones.
- AM at 0 %: **ring mod**. Only the sum and difference tones are left: metallic and hollow.

Slow, it is a tremolo; fast, a new timbre. Reese and robot basses use it.
