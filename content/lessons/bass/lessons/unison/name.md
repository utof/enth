# Unison

Unison stacks copies of the oscillator in every voice, each a little out of tune: the shimmer of a supersaw
and the width of a big bass.

- [Unison](enth:wt1.unison): how many copies (1 is off).
- [Detune](enth:wt1.detune): how far apart; small thickens, large smears.
- [Blend](enth:wt1.blend): the side copies against the center one.
- [Spread](enth:wt1.spread): how wide they sit in stereo.
