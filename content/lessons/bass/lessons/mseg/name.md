# MSEG

An MSEG (multi-segment envelope) is a shape of points you draw: an LFO of any shape, or an envelope.

- [Mode](enth:mseg1.mode): loop (an LFO), one-shot (an envelope) or a loop while the note is held.
- [Sync](enth:mseg1.sync) and [Beats](enth:mseg1.beats): the shape's length in beats of the tempo.
- [Curve 1](enth:mseg1.curve1): bends the first segment; at the top it jumps at once.

Riddim chops, growl vowels and long risers are drawn here.
