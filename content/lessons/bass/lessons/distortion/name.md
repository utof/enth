# Distortion

A distortion bends the wave through a curve, and the bent wave grows new harmonics: buzz, grit and growl.

- [Drive](enth:dist1.drive): how hard the sound hits the curve. More is brighter and rougher.
- [Mode](enth:dist1.mode): the curve. Soft and hard clipping roughen; folds ring and hollow out; bitcrush
  and downsample sound digital.
- [Mix](enth:dist1.mix): blends the clean sound back in, so the body stays under the grit.
