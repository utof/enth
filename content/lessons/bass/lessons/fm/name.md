# FM from B

Bass music's FM: a second oscillator, wt2, is silent ([Level](enth:wt2.level) 0 %) but bends wt1's phase
through an **audio route** into its PM.

- [PM](enth:wt1.pm): where the route lands; a generator dropped on it makes the route.
- [PM index](enth:wt1.pm_index): how far it bends; more is brighter and harsher.
- wt2's [Semi](enth:wt2.semi) and [Fine](enth:wt2.fine): the ratio. 1:1 and 1:2 stay musical; a few
  cents off makes the timbre churn.
