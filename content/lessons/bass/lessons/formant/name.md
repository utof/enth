# The formant filter

A voice makes vowels with two resonances, its formants. The formant filter puts them on any sound, so a bass
can talk.

- [F1](enth:formant1.f1): how open the vowel is, from 'ee' and 'oo' (low) to 'ah' (high).
- [F2](enth:formant1.f2): how far forward, from 'oo' (low) through 'ah' to 'ee' (high).

'a' 850 and 1610 Hz, 'o' 360 and 640, 'ee' 240 and 2400.
