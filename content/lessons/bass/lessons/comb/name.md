# The comb filter

A comb adds the sound to a copy of itself a tiny moment later, over and over: a row of peaks like the teeth
of a comb, on one note and its harmonics. Riddim's metal and colour bass's chords come from here.

- [Feedback](enth:comb1.feedback): how strongly it rings; negative is hollow, an octave down.
- [Semi](enth:comb1.semi): the note it rings at, from the played note.
