# Chorus and flanger

A chorus mixes the sound with copies of itself on slowly moving delays: the copies drift in pitch, and the
sound widens and shimmers. Short delays with feedback make a flanger's jet sweep.

- [Mode](enth:chorus1.mode): chorus, ensemble (three copies a side) or flanger.
- [Rate](enth:chorus1.rate) and [Depth](enth:chorus1.depth): how fast and how far the copies drift.
- [Mix](enth:chorus1.mix): 0 % is the dry sound.
