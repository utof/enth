# The ladder filter

A ladder is the Moog-style low-pass: four stages in a row, so it cuts twice as steeply as the svf and sounds
round and heavy. Pushed, its resonance rings on its own and thins the lows.

- [Cutoff](enth:ladder1.cutoff): where the darkening starts.
- [Resonance](enth:ladder1.resonance): a peak at the cutoff, up to a ringing whistle.
- [Mode](enth:ladder1.mode): 24 or 12 dB/octave low-pass, band-pass or high-pass.
