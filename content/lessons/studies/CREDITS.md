# Credits: the studies pack

Written by the enth contributors (lane DEC, R2), from the lesson plan in [`content/lessons/PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md)
(unit U9). Each study takes apart one of the preset candidates in
[`content/presets-candidates/`](../../presets-candidates/README.md), which agents made with the `enth` CLI; the steps
and their checks were generated by ablation with `cargo xtask lesson-gen`, and the text was edited by hand.
