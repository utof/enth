# Rebuild the filter envelope

Add the [route from envelope 2 to the cutoff](enth:env2->filter1.cutoff) with a large positive depth, and shape envelope 2 like a pluck: a fast [attack](enth:env2.attack), a short [decay](enth:env2.decay) and no [sustain](enth:env2.sustain).

The step passes once the start of each note is as bright as **With**.
