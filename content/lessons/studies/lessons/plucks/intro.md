# Taking Glass Pluck apart

Glass Pluck is a short, bright pluck: a saw with a quiet pulse an octave above it, a filter that opens on every note and closes fast, and a small room around it.

You will rebuild it in four steps, in the order the sound flows:

1. The octave pulse
2. The short decay
3. The filter envelope
4. The room

Each step plays the sound without and with one part, then you put that part back by ear.
