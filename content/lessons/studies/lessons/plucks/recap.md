# What made Glass Pluck

1. **The octave pulse**: a quiet pulse an octave up, for the shine.
2. **The short decay**: no sustain, so every note dies away.
3. **The filter envelope**: a bright start that dulls fast.
4. **The room**: a short, quiet reverb.

Also there from the start, too small to hear on its own: the filter itself, whose envelope does the work, its key tracking, the compressor, and the four macros, wired but at zero.
