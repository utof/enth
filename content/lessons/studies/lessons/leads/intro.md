# Taking Breath Flute apart

Breath Flute imitates a flute with nothing but a synth: a soft triangle tone, breath noise around it, a swell at the start of each note, a gentle vibrato, a slide between notes and a soft hall.

You will rebuild it in seven steps, in the order the sound flows:

1. The breath
2. The triangle
3. The filter
4. The swell
5. The vibrato
6. The slide
7. The hall

Each step plays the sound without and with one part, then you put that part back by ear.
