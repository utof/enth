# What made Breath Flute

1. **The breath**: noise mixed in high.
2. **The triangle**: a soft, hollow tone.
3. **The filter**: the hiss trimmed off.
4. **The swell**: a short attack, like a breath.
5. **The vibrato**: an LFO on the pitch, five times a second.
6. **The slide**: legato with a short glide.
7. **The hall**: a soft reverb.

Also there from the start, too small to hear on a held note: the filter's key tracking, the compressor, and the four macros, wired but at zero.
