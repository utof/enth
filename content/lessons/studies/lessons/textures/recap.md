# What made Wind Tunnel

1. **The band-pass**: a resonant band of noise, for the whistle.
2. **The swell**: a slow rise and a long fade.
3. **The sweep**: a slow LFO moving the band.
4. **The hall**: a long, wet reverb.

Also there from the start, too small to hear: a second, random LFO on the cutoff, the first oscillator switched on at level 0, the compressor, and the four macros, wired but at zero.
