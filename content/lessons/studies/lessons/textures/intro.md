# Taking Wind Tunnel apart

Wind Tunnel is a texture, not a note: white noise through a resonant band-pass filter whose band drifts slowly up and down, rising and fading like a gust, in a long hall.

You will rebuild it in four steps, in the order the sound flows:

1. The band-pass
2. The swell
3. The sweep
4. The hall

Each step plays the sound without and with one part, then you put that part back by ear.
