# Taking Thump Kick apart

Thump Kick is a drum made from a synth voice: a sine wave whose pitch drops fast at the start of each hit, a short body that dies away, and a faint room.

You will rebuild it in four steps, in the order the sound flows:

1. The sine
2. The body
3. The pitch drop
4. The room

Each step plays the sound without and with one part, then you put that part back by ear. A drum is short, so listen closely: everything happens in the first half second.
