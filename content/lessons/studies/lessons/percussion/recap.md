# What made Thump Kick

1. **The sine**: a pure, round tone that starts the same every time.
2. **The body**: a fast fall to silence, no sustain.
3. **The pitch drop**: an envelope on the pitch, for the thump.
4. **The room**: a faint, short reverb.

Also there from the start, too small to hear on a held note: the click (a burst of noise from envelope 2), mono voicing, the filter, the compressor, and the four macros, wired but at zero.
