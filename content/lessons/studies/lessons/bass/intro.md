# Taking Acid Snap apart

Acid Snap is a classic acid bass: one saw through a resonant low-pass filter that snaps shut on every note, with a slide between notes played legato.

You will rebuild it in six steps, in the order the sound flows:

1. The filter
2. Key tracking
3. The drive
4. The amplitude envelope
5. The snap
6. The slide

Each step plays the sound without and with one part, then you put that part back by ear.
