# What made Acid Snap

1. **The filter**: low and resonant, for the dark, nasal tone.
2. **Key tracking**: the cutoff follows the note.
3. **The drive**: a little grit.
4. **The amplitude envelope**: a short release keeps the line tight.
5. **The snap**: an envelope on the cutoff, closing at once.
6. **The slide**: legato with a short glide.

Also there from the start, too small to hear on a held note: the compressor, set as a clean gain stage, and the Bright, Pluck and Drive macros, wired but at zero.
