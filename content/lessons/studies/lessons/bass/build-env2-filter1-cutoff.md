# Rebuild the snap

Add the [route from envelope 2 to the cutoff](enth:env2->filter1.cutoff) and give it a large positive depth. Then shape envelope 2: a fast [attack](enth:env2.attack) and a [sustain](enth:env2.sustain) at 0, so the filter closes again after the snap.

The step passes once each note snaps like **With**.
