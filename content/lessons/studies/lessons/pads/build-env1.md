# Rebuild the slow swell

Lengthen the envelope's [attack](enth:env1.attack) and [release](enth:env1.release) until the note swells in and fades out like the target's; the [decay](enth:env1.decay) and [sustain](enth:env1.sustain) set the level it holds. Press **Target** and **Mine** to compare, then **Submit**.
