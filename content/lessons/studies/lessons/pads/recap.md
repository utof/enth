# What made Velvet Pad

1. **The second saw**: a few cents sharp, so the two beat.
2. **The detune**: the first saw as far flat, for a faster, centred beat.
3. **The filter**: the edge taken off.
4. **The slow swell**: a long attack and a long release.
5. **The hall**: a long, moderate reverb.

Also there from the start, too small to hear on one held note: the filter's key tracking, each note panned by its pitch, the compressor, and the four macros, wired but at zero.
