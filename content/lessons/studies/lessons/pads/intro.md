# Taking Velvet Pad apart

Velvet Pad is a warm pad: two saws tuned slightly apart so they beat slowly against each other, a filter that takes their edge off, a slow swell, and a long hall.

You will rebuild it in five steps, in the order the sound flows:

1. The second saw
2. The detune
3. The filter
4. The slow swell
5. The hall

Each step plays the sound without and with one part, then you put that part back by ear.
