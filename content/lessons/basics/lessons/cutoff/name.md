# The cutoff

The filter shapes the tone after the oscillator. A **low-pass** filter keeps the frequencies below its
[cutoff](enth:filter1.cutoff) and removes the ones above.

- **Lower cutoff:** darker and duller, as if heard through a wall.
- **Higher cutoff:** brighter and buzzier, until the whole saw comes through.

The cutoff is in hertz. Halving it, from 2 kHz to 1 kHz, sounds like one similar step darker wherever you start.
