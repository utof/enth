# Pulse width

A pulse wave switches between high and low. Its [pulse width](enth:osc1.pw) is the share of each cycle spent high.

- At **50 %** it is a square: hollow and woody, odd harmonics only.
- **Narrower** widths add the even harmonics and sound thinner and more nasal, like a reed.

Only the pulse wave reads it. A width and its mirror, 25 % and 75 %, sound the same.
