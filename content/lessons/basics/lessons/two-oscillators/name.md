# Two oscillators

The second oscillator, osc2, sits in the patch switched off; the switch on its card turns it on.

- **[Fine](enth:osc2.fine)** tunes it in cents, hundredths of a semitone. A few cents apart, the two waves drift in
  and out of step: the sound **beats**, a slow wobble, and grows thicker. More cents, faster beats.
- **[Level](enth:osc2.level)** sets how loud it is against the first. Quieter, and the wobble gets shallower.
