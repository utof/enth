# Louder sounds better

Of two sounds, the louder one almost always seems better, fuller and brighter, even when nothing else differs. So when
enth compares sounds, it matches their loudness first.

The [master level](enth:master.level) sets the whole patch's output, in decibels: turn it down to make everything
quieter without changing the tone. Each lesson goes like this one: listen, name what you heard, try it, play a few
rounds, then answer a question or two.
