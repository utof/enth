# Attack and release

env1 is the voice's amplitude envelope: it shapes each note's loudness.

- **[Attack](enth:env1.attack)** is how long the note takes to rise to full level when you press the key. Under
  10 ms it starts at once, like a pluck; over 200 ms it swells in.
- **[Release](enth:env1.release)** is how long it takes to fade to silence after you let go: short stops dead, long
  rings on.
