# Filter modes

The filter's [mode](enth:filter1.mode) chooses which part of the sound around the cutoff it keeps.

- **lp** (low-pass) keeps what is below: darker.
- **hp** (high-pass) keeps what is above: thin, with no body or bass.
- **bp** (band-pass) keeps a band around the cutoff: nasal, like a telephone.
- **notch** removes a band around the cutoff: hollow.

The cutoff sets where each one works.
