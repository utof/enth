# Key tracking

With [key tracking](enth:filter1.keytrack) at 0 %, the cutoff stays put, so high notes, whose harmonics climb past
it, sound duller than low ones. At 100 % the cutoff moves an octave for every octave you play, and every note keeps
the same tone.

The cutoff knob is its value at C4. On one note, key tracking is just a cutoff move: you need two notes to hear it.
