# Drive

[Drive](enth:filter1.drive) pushes the sound into a soft saturator before the filter, then brings the level back
down. It does not make the sound louder: it bends the wave, adding harmonics.

- On a **sine**, which has one harmonic, drive is plain: the pure tone grows a buzzy edge.
- On a **saw**, already full of harmonics, it is subtler: denser and rougher.

More decibels of drive, more grit.
