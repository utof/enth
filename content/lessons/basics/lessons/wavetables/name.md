# Wavetables

The oscillator's fifth wave, **table**, plays a wavetable: a row of waves to move through. [Table](enth:osc1.table)
picks the row, and [Position](enth:osc1.position) where you are in it, from 0 to 100 %.

- **basic:** sine, triangle, saw, square.
- **tilt:** from very bright to nearly a sine.
- **formant:** vowels, A to U.
- **organ:** drawbar organ stops.
- **pulse:** a square narrowing to a thin buzz.
