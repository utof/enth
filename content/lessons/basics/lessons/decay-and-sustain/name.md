# Decay and sustain

The amplitude envelope, env1, shapes each note's loudness over time. After the attack the note reaches full level,
then:

- **[Decay](enth:env1.decay)** is how long it takes to fall from full to the sustain level.
- **[Sustain](enth:env1.sustain)** is the level it holds while the key stays down.

Sustain at 100 % holds like an organ, and the decay does nothing. At 0 % the note dies away: a pluck, and the decay
sets how fast.
