# Four waves

The oscillator makes the raw sound, and its [wave](enth:osc1.wave) sets its colour before anything else touches it.

- **Sine:** one pure harmonic, soft and round.
- **Triangle:** a few odd harmonics that fade fast: mellow, like a flute.
- **Saw:** every harmonic: bright and buzzy.
- **Pulse:** at 50 % a square: hollow and reedy.

Saw and pulse are both bright. The pulse sounds hollower, the saw fuller.
