# Noise

The noise source, noise1, has no pitch at all: it is pure hiss, mixed in with the oscillators. Like osc2, it waits in
the patch switched off until you turn it on.

- **[Color](enth:noise1.color):** **white** has equal energy at every frequency, a bright hiss like steam. **Pink**
  falls away towards the top: softer, like rain.
- **[Level](enth:noise1.level)** sets how much hiss joins the tone. A little adds breath; a lot buries the note.
